# remote-sensing-currents

Surface currents from marine X-band radar and from autonomous surface vehicles (wave gliders): retrieval, calibration and validation — for the Angaur tower radar and the wave-glider ADCPs.

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Amador, Merrifield & Terrill (2023), "Assessment of Atmospheric and Oceanographic Measurements from an Autonomous Surface Vehicle," *JTECH* **40**, 305–326. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-22-0060.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-22-0060.1) | `Amador_2023_Assessment_Measurements_Autonomous_Surface_Vehicle.pdf` | Validates Wave Glider meteorology, waves and ADCP currents against WMO moored buoys, with motion-compensation methods, which bounds the errors of the glider winds and currents used on the bank. |
| Campana, Terrill & de Paolo (2016), "The Development of an Inversion Technique to Extract Vertical Current Profiles from X-Band Radar Observations," *JTECH* **33**, 2015–2028. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-15-0145.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-15-0145.1) | `Campana_2016_Inversion_Vertical_Current_XBand_Radar.pdf` | Recovers near-surface current shear from the wavenumber dependence of radar Doppler shifts, which tells you which depth a radar current represents when comparing it with ADCP and drifter velocities. |
| Hodges et al. (2023), "Evaluation of Ocean Currents Observed from Autonomous Surface Vehicles," *JTECH* **40**, 1121–1136. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-23-0066.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-23-0066.1) | `Hodges_2023_Ocean_Currents_Autonomous_Surface_Vehicles.pdf` | Compares Wave Glider ADCP currents with a bottom-mounted ADCP and introduces a motion-compensation method, giving direct accuracy estimates for the wave-glider current data. |
| Huang, Liu & Gill (2017), "Ocean Wind and Wave Measurements Using X-Band Marine Radar: A Comprehensive Review," *Remote Sens.* **9**, 1261. [doi:10.3390/rs9121261](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs9121261) | `Huang_2017_XBand_Marine_Radar_Wind_Wave_Review.pdf` | Comprehensive review of X-band radar wind, wave and current retrieval methods and their limitations, useful as a single citation for the radar methods section. |
| Lund et al. (2018), "Near-Surface Current Mapping by Shipboard Marine X-Band Radar: A Validation," *JTECH* **35**, 1077–1090. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-17-0154.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-17-0154.1) | `Lund_2018_XBand_Radar_Current_Mapping_Validation.pdf` | Validates X-band radar current maps against dense surface-drifter releases (LASER), a template for checking the tower radar against the Hydrographer Bank drifters. |
| Lund, Graber, Hessner & Williams (2015), "On Shipboard Marine X-Band Radar Near-Surface Current ‘‘Calibration’’," *JTECH* **32**, 1928–1944. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-14-00175.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-14-00175.1) | `Lund_2015_Shipboard_XBand_Radar_Current_Calibration.pdf` | Diagnoses and corrects antenna-heading and range biases in X-band radar currents; the same calibration checks apply to a tower-mounted radar. |
| Young, Rosenthal & Ziemer (1985), "A three‐dimensional analysis of marine radar images for the determination of ocean wave directionality and surface currents," *JGR Oceans* **90**, 1049–1059. [doi:10.1029/jc090ic01p01049](https://doi.org/10.1029/jc090ic01p01049) | `Young_1985_Marine_Radar_Wave_Directionality_Currents.pdf` | Introduced the 3-D (k-omega) spectral fit of the wave dispersion relation to marine-radar image sequences, the basis of X-band surface-current retrieval from the shore tower. |
