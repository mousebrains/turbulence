# epsilon-shear

TKE dissipation (ε) from shear probes: the ATOMIX best-practices and benchmark
papers, the Lueck (2022) statistics that the `epsilonLnSigma` uncertainty model
implements, Goodman coherent-noise removal, and cross-method / cross-instrument
comparison references.

| Ross & Lueck (2003), "Sound scattering from oceanic turbulence," *GRL* **30**(6), 1344. [doi:10.1029/2002GL016733](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GL016733) | `Ross_Lueck_2003_Sound_Scattering_Oceanic_Turbulence.pdf` | The first near-coincident 307 kHz backscatter and microstructure measurements: a forward-looking echosounder on the towed TOMI (four shear probes, two FP07s) over the Knight Inlet sill. Scatter tracks microstructure (r = **0.75** at 1 m range, falling to **0.45** at 20 m). A Goodman-type model driven by shear-probe ε, dT/dz, dS/dz and N predicts scattering strength within **1.2 ± 0.2 dB** with Γ = 0.1, but **−14 to +6 dB** across literature Γ values. Salinity microstructure carries the 307 kHz return for ε ≲ **10⁻⁷ W kg⁻¹**, and turbulence rather than zooplankton made the scatter, which is the premise for acoustic ε (next row). |
| Ross & Lueck (2005), "Estimating turbulent dissipation rates from acoustic backscatter," *Deep-Sea Res. I* **52**(12), 2353–2365. [doi:10.1016/j.dsr.2005.07.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dsr.2005.07.002) | `Ross_Lueck_2005_Estimating_Turbulent_Dissipation_Rates_Acoustic_Backscatter.pdf` | Inverts the same 307 kHz data (431 ten-second points) for ε, given independent dT/dz and dS/dz. Below 4×10⁻⁶ W kg⁻¹ the acoustic ε scales with TOMI's shear-probe ε but averages **0.21×** it (95% CI 0.16–0.29). Above that level the two are uncorrelated, because the negative density ratio (mean R_cρ = −0.75) flattens the scattering-vs-ε curve. The inversion assumes Γ = 0.2 and the Oakey (1982) Batchelor q = 3.7, the same q as `chi/batchelor.py`; Γ = 0.095 brings the ratio to **0.97**. So acoustic ε is not independent of the mixing-efficiency assumption. |
