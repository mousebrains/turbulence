# topographic-wakes

Turbulence, dissipation and mixing in the wakes of headlands, islands and
seamounts: how the bulk Rossby and Froude numbers organize the energetics. This
is the context for the Palau analyses: the Peleliu tip vortex (a headland wake)
and the lee wake of Hydrographer Bank, compared there against LES of stratified
flow past an obstacle.

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Chor & Wenegrat (2025), "The turbulent dynamics of anticyclonic submesoscale headland wakes," *JPO* **55**, 737–759. [doi:10.1175/JPO-D-24-0139.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-D-24-0139.1) | `Chor_Wenegrat_2025_Submesoscale_Headland_Wakes.pdf` | LES with resolved turbulence: KE dissipation, buoyancy mixing and eddy diffusivity scale linearly with the bulk Rossby and Froude numbers (and the slope Burger number) across regimes; regional models may underestimate wake dissipation by up to 10×; centrifugal–symmetric instability in the anticyclonic wake. |
