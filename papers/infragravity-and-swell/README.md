# infragravity-and-swell

Infragravity waves, swell propagation and waves on reefs and islands: generation, trapping and resonance, and the swell-dispersion methods that locate distant storms — for the infragravity modes standing on Hydrographer Bank and their forcing by Typhoon Mawar's swell.

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Ardhuin, Chapron & Collard (2009), "Observation of swell dissipation across oceans," *GRL* **36**, 2008GL037030. [doi:10.1029/2008gl037030](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008gl037030) | `Ardhuin_2009_Swell_Dissipation_Across_Oceans.pdf` | Quantifies swell energy decay with distance and steepness, needed to compare Mawar swell with that from distant storms arriving at Palau. |
| Ardhuin, Rawat & Aucan (2014), "A numerical model for free infragravity waves: Definition and validation at regional and global scales," *Ocean Modell.* **77**, 20–32. [doi:10.1016/j.ocemod.2014.02.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ocemod.2014.02.006) | `Ardhuin_2014_Numerical_Model_Free_Infragravity_Waves.pdf` | Parameterizes coastal free-IG source as a function of incident swell height and period and propagates it globally, giving a framework to estimate remote vs. local IG at the bank. |
| Bertin et al. (2018), "Infragravity waves: From driving mechanisms to impacts," *Earth-Sci. Rev.* **177**, 774–799. [doi:10.1016/j.earscirev.2018.01.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.earscirev.2018.01.002) | `Bertin_2018_Infragravity_Waves_Driving_Mechanisms_Impacts.pdf` | Comprehensive review of IG generation (bound-wave release, breakpoint forcing), propagation, trapping and resonance that frames the bank's 4-40 mHz modes. |
| Cheriton, Storlazzi & Rosenberger (2020), "In situ Observations of Wave Transformation and Infragravity Bore Development Across Reef Flats of Varying Geomorphology," *Front. Mar. Sci.* **7**, 351. [doi:10.3389/fmars.2020.00351](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2020.00351) | `Cheriton_2020_Infragravity_Bore_Development_Reef_Flats.pdf` | Shows how reef-flat width and depth control IG growth, steepening and bores, relevant to how bank-top depth sets IG amplification. |
| Collard, Ardhuin & Chapron (2009), "Monitoring and analysis of ocean swell fields from space: New methods for routine observations," *JGR Oceans* **114**, 2008JC005215. [doi:10.1029/2008jc005215](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008jc005215) | `Collard_2009_Monitoring_Ocean_Swell_Fields_Space.pdf` | SAR swell tracking that projects peak periods forward and backward along great circles at the group velocity, a modern counterpart to Munk/Snodgrass for locating the storms behind the bank's swell. |
| Elgar et al. (1992), "Observations of infragravity waves," *JGR Oceans* **97**, 15573–15577. [doi:10.1029/92jc01316](https://doi.org/10.1029/92jc01316) | `Elgar_1992_Observations_Infragravity_Waves.pdf` | Shows IG energy tracks 7-20 s swell energy and is dominated by free waves except under energetic swell, useful for predicting bank IG levels from Mawar and distant-storm swell. |
| Gawehn et al. (2016), "Identification and classification of very low frequency waves on a coral reef flat," *JGR Oceans* **121**, 7560–7574. [doi:10.1002/2016jc011834](https://doi.org/10.1002/2016jc011834) | `Gawehn_2016_Very_Low_Frequency_Waves_Reef_Flat.pdf` | Classifies VLF reef-flat motions and identifies standing/resonant VLF modes, directly comparable to standing IG modes on the bank. |
| Herbers, Elgar & Guza (1995), "Generation and propagation of infragravity waves," *JGR Oceans* **100**, 24863–24872. [doi:10.1029/95jc02680](https://doi.org/10.1029/95jc02680) | `Herbers_1995_Generation_Propagation_Infragravity_Waves.pdf` | Array observations showing IG directionality tracks incident swell direction, consistent with swell-forced bound waves released as free waves in the surf zone and reflected, the basis for interpreting swell-driven IG on the bank. |
| Herbers, Elgar, Guza & O'Reilly (1995), "Infragravity-Frequency (0.005–0.05 Hz) Motions on the Shelf. Part II: Free Waves," *JPO* **25**, 1063–1079. [doi:10.1175/1520-0485(1995)025<1063:ifhmot>2.0.co;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0485(1995)025<1063:ifhmot>2.0.co;2) | `Herbers_1995_Infragravity_Motions_Shelf_Free_Waves.pdf` | Free IG on shelves (8-204 m) grows with swell energy and decreasing depth, with a depth dependence consistent with refractive trapping, central to identifying trapped modes over the bank. |
| Hoeke et al. (2013), "Widespread inundation of Pacific islands triggered by distant-source wind-waves," *Global and Planetary Change* **108**, 128–138. [doi:10.1016/j.gloplacha.2013.06.006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gloplacha.2013.06.006) | `Hoeke_2013_Pacific_Inundation_Distant_Source_Waves.pdf` | Documents December 2008 inundation of five western Pacific island nations by swell from distant extratropical cyclones, the analog for remote-storm swell forcing at Palau. |
| Pomeroy et al. (2012), "The dynamics of infragravity wave transformation over a fringing reef," *JGR Oceans* **117**, 2012JC008310. [doi:10.1029/2012jc008310](https://doi.org/10.1029/2012jc008310) | `Pomeroy_2012_Infragravity_Wave_Transformation_Fringing_Reef.pdf` | Ningaloo observations of breakpoint IG generation, reflection and standing IG patterns across a reef, a mechanistic analog for the bank. |
| Péquignet, Becker, Merrifield & Aucan (2009), "Forcing of resonant modes on a fringing reef during tropical storm Man‐Yi," *GRL* **36**, 2008GL036259. [doi:10.1029/2008gl036259](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008gl036259) | `Pequignet_2009_Resonant_Modes_Fringing_Reef_ManYi.pdf` | Storm-swell-forced resonant low-frequency modes on a Guam reef flat during Tropical Storm Man-Yi, the closest analog to Mawar-forced standing IG on Hydrographer Bank. |
| Rawat et al. (2014), "Infragravity waves across the oceans," *GRL* **41**, 7957–7963. [doi:10.1002/2014gl061604](https://doi.org/10.1002/2014gl061604) | `Rawat_2014_Infragravity_Waves_Across_Oceans.pdf` | Shows free IG bursts cross whole ocean basins from distant coasts, a possible remote (non-local) IG source at the bank. |
| Roeber & Bricker (2015), "Destructive tsunami-like wave generated by surf beat over a coral reef during Typhoon Haiyan," *Nature Communications* **6**, 7854. [doi:10.1038/ncomms8854](https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms8854) | `Roeber_Bricker_2015_Tsunami_Like_Surf_Beat_Haiyan.pdf` | Typhoon swell groups freed as large IG surf beat over a steep reef, a western Pacific example of extreme typhoon-forced IG. |
| Sheremet, Guza, Elgar & Herbers (2002), "Observations of nearshore infragravity waves: Seaward and shoreward propagating components," *JGR Oceans* **107**. [doi:10.1029/2001jc000970](https://doi.org/10.1029/2001jc000970) | `Sheremet_2002_Nearshore_Infragravity_Waves_Seaward_Shoreward.pdf` | Method to separate incident and reflected IG fluxes, needed to diagnose standing vs. progressive IG on the bank. |
| Smit et al. (2018), "Infragravity Wave Radiation Across the Shelf Break," *JGR Oceans* **123**, 4483–4490. [doi:10.1029/2018jc013986](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018jc013986) | `Smit_2018_Infragravity_Wave_Radiation_Shelf_Break.pdf` | Shows oceanic free IG originates in coastal source regions and that its radiation across the shelf break depends only weakly on shelf geometry, relevant to free IG leaking from or arriving at the bank's flanks. |
| Thomson et al. (2007), "Refraction and reflection of infragravity waves near submarine canyons," *JGR Oceans* **112**, 2007JC004227. [doi:10.1029/2007jc004227](https://doi.org/10.1029/2007jc004227) | `Thomson_2007_Infragravity_Refraction_Submarine_Canyons.pdf` | Observed IG refraction/reflection by steep bathymetry, relevant to IG reflection and trapping at the bank's steep edges. |
