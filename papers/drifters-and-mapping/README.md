# drifters-and-mapping

Drifter-cluster kinematics (vorticity, divergence, strain, their errors) and objective mapping (optimal interpolation, space-time covariances), plus the base-map bathymetry — for the Peleliu tip-vortex drifters and the objective maps of T/S and currents (notes 12, 14).

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Amante & Eakins (2009), "ETOPO1 1 Arc-Minute Global Relief Model: Procedures, Data Sources and Analysis," *NOAA Technical Memorandum NESDIS NGDC-24*, 19 pp. (PDF has 25 pages). [doi:10.7289/v5c8276m](https://doi.org/10.7289/v5c8276m) | `Amante_Eakins_2009_ETOPO1_Global_Relief_Model.pdf` | Source of the regional bathymetry used as the base map for the drifter, objective-map and radar figures around Palau. |
| Huntley et al. (2022), "Conditions for Reliable Divergence Estimates from Drifter Triplets," *JTECH* **39**, 1499–1523. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-21-0161.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-21-0161.1) | `Huntley_2022_Reliable_Divergence_Drifter_Triplets.pdf` | Gives aspect-ratio and interior-angle thresholds for rejecting unreliable divergence/vorticity estimates from drifter triplets, which applies directly to quality-controlling the Peleliu tip-vortex cluster kinematics. |
| Le Traon (1990), "A method for optimal analysis of fields with spatially variable mean," *JGR Oceans* **95**, 13543–13547. [doi:10.1029/jc095ic08p13543](https://doi.org/10.1029/jc095ic08p13543) | `LeTraon_1990_Optimal_Analysis_Spatially_Variable_Mean.pdf` | Estimates a large-scale mean and a mesoscale anomaly together, with a consistent error budget, which is needed when the mean across the bank is not known in advance. |
| Le Traon, Nadal & Ducet (1998), "An Improved Mapping Method of Multisatellite Altimeter Data," *JTECH* **15**, 522–534. [doi:10.1175/1520-0426(1998)015<0522:aimmom>2.0.co;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0426(1998)015<0522:aimmom>2.0.co;2) | `LeTraon_1998_Improved_Mapping_Multisatellite_Altimeter.pdf` | The standard space-time optimal-interpolation scheme with propagating space-time covariance and correlated along-track errors, a model for the space-time covariance used in the ship-track maps. |
| Molinari & Kirwan (1975), "Calculations of Differential Kinematic Properties from Lagrangian Observations in the Western Caribbean Sea," *JPO* **5**, 483–491. [doi:10.1175/1520-0485(1975)005<0483:codkpf>2.0.co;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0485(1975)005<0483:codkpf>2.0.co;2) | `Molinari_Kirwan_1975_Differential_Kinematic_Properties_Lagrangian.pdf` | The original least-squares method for estimating vorticity, divergence and strain from a cluster of drifters, which is the basis of the tip-vortex cluster analysis. |
| Poulain et al. (2023), "Drifter observations of surface currents in the Cyprus Gyre," *Front. Mar. Sci.* **10**, 1266040. [doi:10.3389/fmars.2023.1266040](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2023.1266040) | `Poulain_2023_Drifter_Surface_Currents_Cyprus_Gyre.pdf` | Recent drifter-cluster study of an eddy's kinematics (vorticity and divergence from drifters), a methodological analog for the tip-vortex drifter analysis. |
| Roemmich (1983), "Optimal Estimation of Hydrographic Station Data and Derived Fields," *JPO* **13**, 1544–1549. [doi:10.1175/1520-0485(1983)013<1544:oeohsd>2.0.co;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0485(1983)013<1544:oeohsd>2.0.co;2) | `Roemmich_1983_Optimal_Estimation_Hydrographic_Station_Data.pdf` | Applies objective analysis to irregularly spaced hydrographic data and derived fields, with covariance estimated from the data, as for the ship T/S and ADCP maps. |
| Spydell, Feddersen & Macmahan (2019), "The Effect of Drifter GPS Errors on Estimates of Submesoscale Vorticity," *JTECH* **36**, 2101–2119. [doi:10.1175/jtech-d-19-0108.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jtech-d-19-0108.1) | `Spydell_2019_Drifter_GPS_Errors_Vorticity.pdf` | Quantifies how GPS position error and a narrow cluster (small minor axis) inflate vorticity errors to O(5f), which sets the error budget for the Peleliu tip-vortex vorticity. |
| Zeiden et al. (2022), "Vorticity in the Wake of Palau from Lagrangian Surface Drifters," *JPO* **52**, 2237–2255. [doi:10.1175/jpo-d-21-0252.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jpo-d-21-0252.1) | `Zeiden_2022_Vorticity_Wake_Palau_Drifters.pdf` | Drifter-cluster vorticity estimates (least squares and wavelet) of submesoscale wake eddies up to 6f off Palau, the closest published analog to the Peleliu tip vortex. |
