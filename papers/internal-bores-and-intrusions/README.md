# internal-bores-and-intrusions

Internal bores, internal-tide upwelling and cold intrusions onto reefs, banks and shelves, plus the water-mass tools used to trace them — the context for cold water from the top of the pycnocline repeatedly lifted onto the Hydrographer Bank crest (notes 15).

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Davis et al. (2020), "Fate of Internal Waves on a Shallow Shelf," *JGR Oceans* **125**, e2019JC015377. [doi:10.1029/2019jc015377](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019jc015377) | `Davis_etal_2020_Fate_Internal_Waves_Shallow_Shelf.pdf` | Tracks internal-wave transformation, dissipation and bore/bolus formation on a shallow reef shelf, the process chain behind cold-water emplacement on the crest. |
| Leichter et al. (2012), "The island‐scale internal wave climate of Moorea, French Polynesia," *JGR Oceans* **117**, 2012JC007949. [doi:10.1029/2012jc007949](https://doi.org/10.1029/2012jc007949) | `Leichter_etal_2012_Island_Scale_Internal_Wave_Climate_Moorea.pdf` | Multi-year record of semidiurnal internal-wave cold surges with upslope flow and vertical shear on Pacific island reef slopes: the regional analog for the crest's tidally phased cold lifts. |
| MacKinnon & Gregg (2003), "Mixing on the Late-Summer New England Shelf—Solibores, Shear, and Stratification," *JPO* **33**, 1476–1492. [doi:10.1175/1520-0485(2003)033<1476:motlne>2.0.co;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0485(2003)033<1476:motlne>2.0.co;2) | `MacKinnon_Gregg_2003_Mixing_Late_Summer_New_England_Shelf.pdf` | Solibores plus low-mode shear setting dissipation in a stratified shelf. It is the closest analog for the bore-driven dense layer and Ri ~0.25-0.4 shear bands on the crest. |
| Sinnett et al. (2018), "Observations of Nonlinear Internal Wave Run-Up to the Surfzone," *JPO* **48**, 531–554. [doi:10.1175/jpo-d-17-0210.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/jpo-d-17-0210.1) | `Sinnett_etal_2018_Nonlinear_Internal_Wave_Runup_Surfzone.pdf` | Detailed run-up of nonlinear internal waves and bores into very shallow water, showing how dense cold fronts climb a slope like the 40-60 m lifts onto the crest. |
| Storlazzi et al. (2020), "Internal tides can provide thermal refugia that will buffer some coral reefs from future global warming," *Scientific Reports* **10**, 13435. [doi:10.1038/s41598-020-70372-9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-70372-9) | `Storlazzi_etal_2020_Internal_Tides_Thermal_Refugia_Reefs.pdf` | Global view of where internal-tide cooling reaches reefs. It frames the Palau bank intrusions as part of a broader internal-tide thermal-refugia pattern. |
