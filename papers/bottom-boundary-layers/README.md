# bottom-boundary-layers

Turbulence in bottom boundary layers over rough beds and reefs: Reynolds stress,
drag, the log layer and dissipation from point-velocity and acoustic instruments.
This is the context for the moored MicroRider / Signature work on the Hydrographer
Bank crest (stress, C_D and near-bed ε).

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Reidenbach, Monismith, Koseff, Yahel & Genin (2006), "Boundary layer turbulence and flow structure over a fringing coral reef," *Limnol. Oceanogr.* **51**, 1956–1968. [doi:10.4319/lo.2006.51.5.1956](https://doi.org/10.4319/lo.2006.51.5.1956) | `Reidenbach_2006_Coral_Reef_Boundary_Layer.pdf` | Stress, drag and dissipation over a rough coral reef from ADVs: the reef-boundary-layer reference for near-bed stress and drag over the crest. |
| Kirincich, Lentz & Gerbi (2010), "Calculating Reynolds Stresses from ADCP Measurements in the Presence of Surface Gravity Waves Using the Cospectra-Fit Method," *JTECH* **27**, 889–907. [doi:10.1175/2009jtecho682.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/2009jtecho682.1) | `Kirincich_etal_2010_Reynolds_Stresses_ADCP_Cospectra_Fit.pdf` | Wave-bias removal for ADCP Reynolds stress (cospectra fit), needed to trust the crest stress profiles that define the ~8 m bottom boundary layer. |
| Rosman & Hench (2011), "A framework for understanding drag parameterizations for coral reefs," *JGR* **116**, C08025. [doi:10.1029/2010jc006892](https://doi.org/10.1029/2010jc006892) | `Rosman_Hench_2011_Drag_Parameterizations_Coral_Reefs.pdf` | Reconciles reef drag coefficients, roughness lengths and reference heights, needed to interpret the crest's measured stress and bottom-boundary-layer drag. |
