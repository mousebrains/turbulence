# stratified-turbulence-anisotropy

Isotropy criteria and the structure of stratified turbulence — the backdrop for
the VMP two-probe isotropy/anisotropy investigation (the Re_b U-curve, the
length-scale ratios, and the ε/νN² isotropy threshold).

| Paper | File | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Gargett, Osborn & Nasmyth (1984), *JFM* **144**, 231–280. [doi:10.1017/S0022112084001592](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0022112084001592) | `Gargett_1984_Local_Isotropy_Stratified_Decay.pdf` | The isotropy benchmark: spectral isotropy requires ε/νN² ≳ 200; defines the decay/anisotropy regimes our Re_b U-curve should be read against. |
| Yamazaki & Osborn (1990), *JGR* **95**(C6), 9739–9744. [doi:10.1029/JC095iC06p09739](https://doi.org/10.1029/JC095iC06p09739) | `Yamazaki_Osborn_1990_Dissipation_Stratified.pdf` | Isotropic ε formula usable for Re_b > 20 — the classic threshold for two-probe work. |
| Itsweire, Koseff, Briggs & Ferziger (1993), *JPO* **23**, 1508–1522. [doi:10.1175/1520-0485(1993)023<1508:TISSFI>2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0485%281993%29023%3C1508:TISSFI%3E2.0.CO;2) | `Itsweire_1993_Stratified_Shear_Flows_Mixing.pdf` | DNS of stratified shear flows: how shear-probe-style estimates degrade with buoyancy — context for anisotropy at low Re_b. |
| Smyth & Moum (2000), "Anisotropy of turbulence in stably stratified mixing layers," *Phys. Fluids* **12**, 1343–1362. [doi:10.1063/1.870386](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.870386) | `Smyth_Moum_2000_Anisotropy_Stratified_Mixing_Layers.pdf` | Anisotropy of dissipation-range turbulence in stratified mixing layers — length-scale ratios we can mirror with L_T/L_O. |
| Smyth & Moum (2000), "Length scales of turbulence in stably stratified mixing layers," *Phys. Fluids* **12**, 1327–1342. [doi:10.1063/1.870385](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.870385) | `Smyth_Moum_2000_Length_Scales_Stratified.pdf` | The L_T/L_O/L_E foundations — companion to the Anisotropy paper; underpins the L_T-adaptive dissipation-window controller. |
| Smyth & Moum (2000), "Ocean turbulence," unpublished extended manuscript (last revised September 2000), College of Oceanic & Atmospheric Sciences, Oregon State University. It extends their "Three-dimensional (3D) turbulence," *Encyclopedia of Ocean Sciences*, Academic Press (2001), 2947–2955. No DOI for this version; the published article is [doi:10.1006/rwos.2001.0134](https://doi.org/10.1006/rwos.2001.0134). | `Smyth_Moum_2000_Ocean_Turbulence.pdf` | A tutorial overview, distinct from the two Smyth & Moum (2000) *Phys. Fluids* papers above. It covers the K41 and Batchelor spectra (k_K = (ε/ν³)^{1/4}, k_B = (ε/νκ²)^{1/4}, Pr ≈ 7, C_K ≈ **1.6**). It also shows how shear (Corrsin scale L_C = (ε/S³)^{1/2}), stratification (Ozmidov scale L_O = (ε/N³)^{1/2}) and boundaries each break isotropy. Below L_O, fluctuations "differ little" from isotropic turbulence; between L_O and the internal waves lies a "murky" band of nonlinear waves and anisotropic turbulence. That makes it a plain-language frame for reading the Re_b U-curve. |
| Rehmann & Hwang (2005), "Small-scale structure of strongly stratified turbulence," *JPO* **35**, 151–164. [doi:10.1175/JPO-2676.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-2676.1) | `Rehmann_Hwang_2005_SmallScale_Stratified_Turbulence.pdf` | Rapid-distortion analysis of weak turbulence in strong stratification: isotropy-based ε formulas can be wrong by >3× under mean shear — a caution for the two-probe isotropy null and the ε/νN² threshold. (Rockland distributes this as TN-016; filed here as the paper.) |
| Klymak & Moum (2007), "Oceanic isopycnal slope spectra. Part II: Turbulence," *JPO* **37**(5), 1232–1245. [doi:10.1175/JPO3074.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO3074.1) | `Klymak_Moum_2007_Oceanic_Isopycnal_Slope_Spectra_II_Turbulence.pdf` | Towed-body (Marlin, ~700 m, Hawaiian Ridge) thermistor spectra compared with shear-probe ε over a four-decade range of ε. Horizontal slope spectra follow ε^{2/3}k_x^{1/3} out to wavelengths beyond **100 m**, far past the Ozmidov scale, so those scales are anisotropic turbulence. Fitting the spectral level with Γ = 0.2 (an "inverse of the Osborn method") gives thermistor ε that is unbiased on average. Over **9059** 32-m spectra, however, r = **0.56** against the shear probes, and single blocks can be off by more than 10×. Their summed-subrange model needed q = **2.3**, below the 3.7 in `chi/batchelor.py`. |
| Kunze (2019), "A unified model spectrum for anisotropic stratified and isotropic turbulence in the ocean and atmosphere," *JPO* **49**(2), 385–407. [doi:10.1175/JPO-D-18-0092.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-D-18-0092.1) | `Kunze_2019_Unified_Model_Spectrum_Anisotropic_Stratified_Isotropic.pdf` | Uses dimensional scaling to model the decade below k_O = (N³/ε)^{1/2} as one forward cascade at constant ε. Anisotropic stratified turbulence (aspect ratio ~f/N rising to 1) feeds isotropic turbulence above k_O. The isotropic band has bandwidth k_K/k_O ~ Re_b^{3/4} and is expected to vanish for Re_b < O(**100**). Kunze also argues, and calls uncertain himself, that pycnocline Γ ≈ **0.2** at high Re_b because overturns reach L_O, while near boundaries and in labs or DNS L_O exceeds the domain and Γ drops. That bears on both the two-probe Re_b U-curve and Γ in bottom-boundary-layer data. |
